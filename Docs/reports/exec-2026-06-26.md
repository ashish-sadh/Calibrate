1. **The finite-epic discipline delivered.** A testable checklist (#900) drove both P0 routing whiffs to closed → verified → shipped in ~1 day, then handed off to a scoped polish phase (#909) rather than lingering as an open-ended "audit until no whiff remains."
2. **DriftChatSim is earning its keep.** The chat-sim harness surfaced the concrete whiffs (#896 / #897 / #898) that became the testable work — replacing speculation with reproducible failures.
3. **Planning rigor caught a self-inflicted risk.** A principal-engineer validation pass on the draft polish epic found 3 Done-When verifiers that would have passed *before any work landed* (they matched pre-existing tests); they were re-gated on net-new named tests, and the lesson was recorded as a durable planning sign so it can't recur.

## Risks & Blockers
1. **Premium-feel-under-failure is the remaining launch gap.** The Nebius turn has no in-flight timeout (#890) — the spinner can hang indefinitely; offline-on-first-turn shows a bare error string, not a calm retry (#891). These two **gate the polish epic #909** with a 3-cycle escalation to operator review.
2. **Multi-item Indian meals may drop items (#898).** "paneer butter masala with 2 naan" can collapse to one row — a dropped naan = a wrong calorie total the user sees on the dashboard. A bounded ≤60-min spike runs first to determine whether the iOS review sheet recovers the items (parse-polish) or also drops them (escalates to P0 co-showstopper).
3. **Harness reliability: `is-clean-state.sh` intermittently hangs (#907).** Caused two **benign** senior crashes this period (no data loss) and burns session budget on false "clean: false." Tracked as a routine fix.
4. **`Docs/state.md` is stale** (reads Build 234; actual 306) — a known manual-snapshot drift; live build/test state lives in git + the sprint cache, not this doc.
